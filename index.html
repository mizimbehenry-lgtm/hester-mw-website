ABOUT HESTER MW

Music from Malawi. A sound with no limits.

Hester Mw is a Malawian Afropiano artist, singer and songwriter bringing together Afropiano, Afrofusion, Afrobeat and Amapiano influences.

His music combines rhythm, emotion and storytelling to create songs that connect with people and celebrate African creativity.
