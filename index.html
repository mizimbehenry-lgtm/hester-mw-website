ABOUT HESTER MW

Sound with
a story.

Hester Mw is a Malawian artist, singer and songwriter building a distinctive sound around Afropiano, Afro-fusion, Afrobeat and dance-driven Amapiano influences.

With a focus on rhythm, melody and meaningful expression, Hester Mw creates music that connects personal stories with contemporary African sounds.

This website is the official digital home of Hester Mw — a place for music, videos, new releases, performances, collaborations and opportunities to connect.
